How grades work

Each player starts from his DynastyProcess dynasty value, adjusted for how he's scoring in this league this season and any injury. Draft picks use DynastyProcess pick values, with next year's picks projected from the original team's current standing. Playoff teams pick 7 through 12 in reverse order of the standings. The six non-playoff teams go through the draft lottery, so their picks are valued as the average of every pick they could land, weighted by their lottery odds.

Value is split into win-now and long-term based on age and position. Contenders weigh win-now value more, and rebuilders weigh long-term value more, so the same trade can grade differently for each team.

A "stud tax" means one great player is worth more than several good ones adding up to the same total. The grade comes from how much value each team gains after all of that: A is a steal (or a strong deal for both teams), C is fair, and F is a massive overpay or undersell.
